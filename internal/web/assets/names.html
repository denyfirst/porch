The names
your certificates
have already published.

A bare domain. No name is invented and nothing is sent to your hosts: the names come from a public register of certificates, published by whoever obtained them, and from the mail, sender policy and delegation records your domain publishes for anybody to read. Where this installation can be reached by anybody but you, it produces an inventory only for a domain you have proven control of — it lists an estate rather than measuring a host, and the party being listed cannot see it happen. What is asked, and what it cannot show is written out in full.
