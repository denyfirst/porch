Where this goes at your DNS provider

Every provider asks for the same three things: the type, TXT; the name; and the value, pasted as it is. Most add your domain to the name themselves, so type only the part before it: for _porch-challenge.www.example.com in the zone example.com, that is _porch-challenge.www.

If the record reads back with your domain on it twice, the provider added it for you: type the shorter form. Menus move more often than the form changes.

This is help with a form, not part of the proof. The record is looked for on whichever servers your zone names, whoever runs them: control of the zone is what is proven, and a zone moved to another provider is still yours.
