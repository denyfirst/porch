# Porch

> Porch checks what a server shows the outside world (TLS, the website, mail and DNS) and grades it against published standards. It is open source under AGPL-3.0, runs on your own server, and is made by denyfirst.

- Every finding links the RFC or guideline it rests on, and every report says what it could not measure.
- A copy checks a domain only after that domain publishes a TXT record proving control.
- Nothing intrusive: no exploits, no credential guessing, nothing malformed, no port sweeps and no private addresses. The one port check is a fixed list of services that should never face the internet, one connection each, with nothing sent.
- No account, no telemetry, no log of who checked what. The service keeps results only encrypted under its password; the command line keeps none.
- Go's standard library only. Releases are signed and can be rebuilt byte for byte.
- This site runs Porch against denyfirst's own domain only. To check your own, run a copy yourself.
