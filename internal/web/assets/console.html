Where this goes at your DNS provider

Every provider asks for the same three things: the type, TXT; the name; and the value, pasted as it is. Most add your domain to the name themselves, so type only the part before it: for _porch-challenge.www.example.com in the zone example.com, that is _porch-challenge.www.
