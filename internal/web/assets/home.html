02 / How we work

Three rules.

We hold ourselves to them, and you can check each one.

  1. 01

    Evidence first.

    Every result shows what was seen and the standard behind it.

    How you check it Each finding in a report links the RFC or guideline it rests on.

  2. 02

    Your data stays with you.

    Our tools run on your machine. We collect nothing.

    How you check it Open your browser’s network panel: these pages load nothing from anyone else.

  3. 03

    A clear scope.

    A check reads what a server already shows, and nothing more.

    How you check it docs/checks.md lists every connection a scan makes.
