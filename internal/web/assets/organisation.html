This site

No cookies, no analytics, and nothing loaded from other sites. It runs on the same server as Porch’s demo, rented from Hetzner Online GmbH in Germany. The server keeps no log of visits; what it does keep is on Porch’s privacy page.

Writing to us

A letter to hello@denyfirst.dev reaches our mail provider and us like any other mail, with the address and headers it was sent with. We use it to answer you and for nothing else: it goes on no list, and it is not passed on. Write from any address you like. Security reports go to the address in security.txt.
